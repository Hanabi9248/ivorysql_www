The IvorySQL Campus Tour visits Shandong University's Software Park Campus on October 22, 2026 (Thursday), from 08:30 to 11:30 (China Standard Time, UTC+8). Co-hosted by IvorySQL and the China Open Source Database Alliance (COSDA), the event connects database technology, industry practice, and career development under the theme “From PostgreSQL to AI.”

This event is presented in Chinese for Shandong University students in computer science and related disciplines. Speakers 魏波, 王孝鹏, 孙重吉, 石卓妍, 怀玉杰, and 苑立群 will discuss PostgreSQL, IvorySQL, PGNexus, and HighgoBase, exploring engineering practice, open-source collaboration, and data services for AI applications.

For speaker introductions, the full agenda, and the event poster, visit the [Chinese event page](/zh/community/events/event-202618).
