On September 12, 2026, the PostgreSQL & IvorySQL Technical Meetup · Hangzhou was held at Yungu Fudi Center, bringing together DBAs, developers, architects, and AI engineers from Hangzhou and nearby cities for six talks spanning the PostgreSQL kernel, high availability, memory troubleshooting, enterprise RAG, and AI sandbox engineering.

Speakers: 德哥, 唐成, 付超, Nickyoung, 吴建波, and 马顺华. Replay videos and the full recap are available on the [Chinese event page](/zh/community/events/event-202615).
